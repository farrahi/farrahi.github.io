The Data Analytics in Sport Science research group is broadly interested in applied machine learning and data science, with a focus on applications related to movement, sports, and health.

## Research Topics

- Wearable-measured movement behavior and health
- Wearable accelerometry
- Activity recognition
- Compositional time-use data analysis
- Movement behavior profile analysis
- Data-driven health
- Deep learning
- Machine learning

## Emerging Research Topics

- Large Language Models and their applications to health and wearable data
- Foundation models for wearables
- Quantified self
- Causal machine learning
